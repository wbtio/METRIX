# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals committed to strict personal growth, tracking goals quantitatively with points and rigorous discipline.

## Product Purpose
METRIX is a strict, point-based system to plan, track, and score personal goals. It replaces casual to-do lists with structured accountability, Gemini-evaluated daily progress logging, and competitive challenges.

## Brand Personality
- **Strict & Accountable**: Uncompromising, focused on real performance.
- **Cosmic & Futuristic**: High-fidelity dark mode with deep teal highlights, glowing indicators, and stellar design accents.
- **Minimal & Decisive**: Fast transitions, clean hierarchy, minimal fluff.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-cream typical templates (plain navy/gray, boring grids).
- Playful bounce animations that feel childish.
- Decorative-only motion that slows down interaction.

## Design Principles
- **Motion as feedback**: Animation only occurs to signal state change (check-ins, logging success, panels expanding).
- **Tabular & Structured**: Data-heavy, precise numeric alignments (tabular-nums).
- **RTL-Native Excellence**: Flawless bilingual Arabic and English layout and flows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` at all levels.
- High-contrast text alignments.
